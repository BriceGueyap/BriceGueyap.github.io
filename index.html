Welcome to my home page!

I am a Ph.D. Candidate (ABD) in the Department of Economics at Western University.
My research is in econometric theory, with applications to network and panel data.
I work on identification and estimation in semiparametric models where unobserved heterogeneity drives both outcomes and the formation of links between agents.
I also do applied work on institutions, trust and political representation in Africa.

I am on the job market for the 2026-2027 academic year.

Econometrics (theory and applied) -- social networks;
